KEY ANALYTICAL FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Bus rush-hour average delay stays within 12.18-12.52 minutes across the 06-09 and 16-19 peaks - steady rather than spiking.
2. Largest bus bottleneck: Brooklyn / Heavy Traffic with 14,556.27 passenger-hours lost.
3. Largest train bottleneck: East of Hudson / Signal Problem with 37,009.68 passenger-hours lost.
4. Cached Spark SQL was 8.46x faster than the raw CSV scan.
5. Partitioned Parquet was 3.75x vs raw CSV on this run.
6. Parquet reduced the stored footprint by 70.7%.
7. Staten Island showed the highest single-day bus average delay (57.23 min).

CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spark SQL successfully executed hourly, spatial and windowed analytics over 100,000 unified records. The physical plan confirms projection pushdown and partition-local partial aggregation before the shuffle. Caching delivered the largest query speedup, while partitioned Parquet delivered a large storage reduction and provides the optimized artifacts consumed by Experiment 6.
