KEY FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Compound indexes turn repeated corridor/reason lookups into targeted index scans.
2. The optimizer selects the composite prefix that matches equality filters and sort order.
3. Documents examined - not just latency - prove the index is doing real work.
4. Aggregations over severe delays reuse idx_delay_desc and return in one pass.
5. Index storage is a small fraction of the 100,000-document lake.

CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 100,000-record MongoDB data lake was ingested with chunked bulk writes, indexed on mode/location/delay, reason/delay, mode/hour/date and delay, and benchmarked against the same queries before indexing. Every benchmark query returned identical results with fewer documents examined and lower latency, confirming that the indexes are effective rather than merely present.
